Extra hardening in the secure build

  • Logout that sticks. Sign out, press Back or paste the dashboard URL: you land on sign-in. Pages are sent with Cache-Control: no-store and the session id is deleted on the server, so even a copied cookie stops working.
  • CSRF tokens. Every form carries a random token. A POST without it gets 400.
  • Login throttling. Five wrong passwords for one account pauses sign-in for five minutes (HTTP 429).
  • Security headers. CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, nosniff and Referrer-Policy on every response.
  • Session fixation. The session is replaced with a new one at sign-in.
  • Encrypted email. Email addresses are encrypted at rest with a separate key from the session cookie.

Every check on this page is automated in tests/test_security.py. Run python -m unittest -v to attack both builds at once.
